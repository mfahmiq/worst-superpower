# Worst Superpower — candidates and tournament

Competition: `u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe` ("Worst Superpower"), $2, free entry.
Deadline: 2026-11-26T17:30:00Z. Gate: `entry.txt` UTF-8, 1–140 characters, not only whitespace.

Judge brief (weight order, from RULES.txt):
1. Genuinely useless superpower, stated in one line; a real/useful/merely-weak power loses.
2. The uselessness lands as a joke: the reader pictures the power in use and laughs at how little it achieves.
3. Unexpected: not a familiar gag (slow flight, invisible while nobody watches).
4. Tight and specific, with a concrete detail fixing the power in the world.
5. Clear on a single reading, needing no setup beyond the sentence itself.
Also: a power that is really a curse, or that quietly helps, is not a useless superpower.
Tiebreak rule in the brief: go through the criteria in order, let the first difference decide,
unless a later one differs by much more.

## Champion

> **I can see five seconds into the future, but only of myself blinking.** (68 characters)

Why it wins, in the brief's own order: (1) precognition of your own blink is useless under every
reading; (2) the seer solemnly announcing his own blink is a scene you see in one second and laugh
at; (3) the combination is new — the power is famous, the limitation absurdly specific, never
paired before; (4) "five seconds" and "blinking" are exact, physical, concrete details; (5) one
sentence, one setup clause, one punchline, clear on a single reading.
